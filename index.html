Western Pennsylvania · 1850

Telegraph Frontier

The railroad is coming and every town on the map wants the news. Run wire from your home office, open an office in each village, and watch the settlements grow once the line reaches them.

  • SurveyTap a village to see its size, trade and traffic.
  • Lay wireTap where the line starts, then where it ends. The route is planned for you. Keep tapping to carry the run on from where it stopped. Esc or right-click to start somewhere new.
  • OfficesA village gets its office once its wire is joined to your home office. Wire that is not joined yet earns nothing.
  • TerrainPoles are cheap along the railroad, dearer through forest and hills, dearest across rivers.
  • MoneyFunds buy poles. Every village on the line adds income, and it grows as the village grows.
  • MoveDrag to pan, wheel or pinch to zoom. Space pauses, 1 to 3 set speed.
