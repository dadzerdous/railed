Runes of Midgard

The old paths have gone dark. From your Rune Tower, carve a new network across Midgard. Every square matters: terrain consumes resources, ancient sites strengthen your network, and settlements offer different rewards.

  • SurveyInspect settlements and resource sites before choosing where to expand.
  • Carve RunesStart on your connected network, then place the route yourself one square at a time. No automatic pathfinding.
  • ResourcesSilver pays workers. Timber builds runestones. Rune Power bends difficult terrain and crosses rivers.
  • SitesRun a connected path through Groves, Silver Veins and Rune Springs to gain passive resources.
  • SettlementsDifferent settlements produce different resources once joined to your rune network.
  • GoalFor now: grow a strong network. The Bifröst comes after we prove this loop is fun.
